Klamath Tribes

The Three Peoples

The Klamath Tribes is a confederation of three distinct but related peoples who have lived in the Klamath Basin of south-central Oregon and northern California since time immemorial.

The Three Peoples

Klamath

The Klamath people (Eukshikni Maqlaqsanak) lived around Upper Klamath Lake and the Klamath River. Their name means "People of the Lake."

Modoc

The Modoc (Móatokni Maqlaqsanak) lived around Tule Lake and Lower Klamath Lake in California. Known for the Modoc War of 1872–73.

Yahooskin

The Yahooskin Band of Snake Paiute joined the confederation. They were semi-nomadic people of the high desert to the east.

About the Klamath Language

The Klamath language (also called Klamath-Modoc) is a language isolate — it is not demonstrably related to any other language family. It is spoken natively by very few elders and is considered critically endangered.

The Klamath Tribes Cultural Programs department works to revitalize the language through education programs, recordings, and resources like this app.

The Medicine Wheel

The Medicine Wheel is a sacred symbol for many Plains and Plateau peoples. Its four quadrants represent directions, seasons, and aspects of life:

East — Yellow

New beginnings, spring, youth

South — Red

Growth, summer, adulthood

West — Black

Introspection, autumn, maturity

North — White

Wisdom, winter, elders
